# Project Overview

ShopNear is an AI-powered local marketplace for discovering nearby businesses, products, and services.

## Roles

1. Customer — discovers listings, saves favorites, chats, and receives notifications.
2. Business — registers a business, manages its profile and products, receives messages, and views analytics.
3. Service Provider — registers professional services, portfolio media, availability, and service area.
4. Administrator — manages users, verification, reports, suspension, and platform settings.

## Phase 1

The existing responsive marketplace UI remains intact: Home, Search, Favorites, Messages, Profile, AI search entry, business/product/service discovery, and ShopNear branding.

## Phase 2

Phase 2 establishes the identity and account foundation: phone login, development OTP, role selection, profile setup, secure sessions, role authorization, business/provider registration, protected uploads, and “New on ShopNear”.
